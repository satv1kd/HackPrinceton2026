Calibration · about 3 minutes

Read a little.
Teach us a lot.

You’ll read ten short sentences out loud while we record them. Together they carry every sound in English — which is how we start learning the way you say things.

  • Nothing is graded. There is no wrong way to sound.
  • You can pause, skip a sentence, or stop at any time.
  • Recordings go only to Heard running on your own machine.
